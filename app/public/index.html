Starbound Run

Race through the stage, answer questions, and reach the finish flag.

How it works

  • Run and jump through the stage.
  • Question gates block your path: answer correctly to open them.
  • Faster answers mean a faster finish, because the race clock never stops.
  • Falling or getting hurt costs a heart; at 0 hearts you answer a revive question.
  • First to the finish flag wins. Host a room for a classroom race.
